📋 Material Review Blueprint (Kisi-Kisi)

  • Asexual Reproduction in Plants: Definitions, advantages/disadvantages, vegetative propagation structures (rhizomes, stem tubers, cuttings, runners).
  • Sexual Reproduction in Plants: Flower structure and functions (petals, sepals, stamen, anther, filament, carpel/pistil, stigma, style, ovary, ovule, receptacle), pollination vs fertilization, seed and fruit formation.
  • Human Male Reproduction: Organ functions (testes, scrotum, prostate, urethra), sperm cell structure (head, acrosome, nucleus, midpiece, mitochondria, tail/flagellum) and chromosome count (haploid = 23).
  • Human Female Reproduction: Organ functions (ovary, oviduct/fallopian tube, uterus, endometrium, cervix, vagina), fertilisation site, embryo implantation, comparison of sperm vs egg cells.
  • Menstrual Cycle & Puberty: Menstrual cycle timeline calculation (ovulation calculation), menopause definition, secondary sexual characteristics during puberty, and STIs (HIV).
